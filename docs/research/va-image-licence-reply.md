# V&A reply: use of three collection images in the study

This is the reply from the V&A Images team (vaimages@vam.ac.uk) to `va-image-licence-request.md`. The project lead forwarded it, and it is reproduced verbatim. Decision record DR-020 lists the conditions and how the platform enforces them.

---

Dear Saurav Kumar, Ankit Kumar and Professor Priyadarshi Patnaik,

Thank you for contacting the Victoria and Albert Museum and for providing a detailed explanation of your proposed research study at the Centre of Excellence for Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur.

We appreciate your efforts to make knowledge of Indian textile traditions more accessible through innovative digital learning environments.

Based on the information provided, we are pleased to confirm that the proposed use of the following three collection images may be treated as **non-commercial academic and educational use under Section 3 of our image-use terms**, subject to the conditions outlined in your request:

1. **5473A/(IS) (O469296):** Pieces of an ajrakh textile, Sind.
2. **IS.1765-1883 (O164855):** Textile incorporating kalamkari and block printing, Machilipatnam.
3. **4497(IS) (O481213):** Wrapped garment, probably Sanganer.

Our understanding is that the images will be used exclusively within a non-commercial academic research study comparing a conventional web-based learning environment with an interactive virtual-museum environment.

For the purposes of this proposed use, we would regard the virtual-museum environment as an academic digital learning interface rather than a commercial exhibition, display or catalogue, provided that it remains within the scope described in your correspondence.

We have no objection to the images being presented in both versions of the study, including the associated public research demonstration, on the following basis:

- The images are reproduced at a maximum resolution of 768 pixels on their longest side.
- Each image is accompanied by the acknowledgement **© Victoria and Albert Museum, London**.
- The images are used solely for the stated non-commercial research, educational and demonstration purposes.
- The proposed period of use does not exceed five years from the date of first publication.
- The images are not used in advertising, promotional materials, commercial products or revenue-generating activities.
- The minor cropping described, solely to remove the museum's paper identification tag, is acceptable provided that it does not materially alter or misrepresent the collection object.

We also acknowledge that the same images will be presented in both learning environments to maintain consistency in the research methodology.

**Accordingly, no separate commercial exhibition licence would be required for the specific non-commercial academic use described in your request**, provided that the applicable image-use terms continue to be observed and any relevant third-party rights are respected.

Should the scope of the project change substantially, particularly in relation to commercial distribution, promotional use or the establishment of a separately operated public exhibition, we would ask that you contact us for further guidance.

We wish you and your colleagues at IIT Kharagpur every success with this research initiative and look forward to seeing how digital environments can contribute to the study and appreciation of textile heritage.
