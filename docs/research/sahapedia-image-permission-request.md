# Request to Sahapedia: use of a gallery photograph in a non-commercial research study

**To:** contact@sahapedia.org
**From:** cxa.craftecosystem@gmail.com
**Subject:** Non-commercial academic use of a Kalamkari gallery photograph in a learning study (IIT Kharagpur)

Dear Sahapedia team,

We are a research team at the Centre of Excellence for Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. Our study (ethics approval CXA/ETH/01) compares two ways of presenting knowledge about Indian hand-block-printed textiles to learners:

- a conventional web page;
- a virtual museum that participants walk through on a computer screen.

The platform is non-commercial. Nothing is sold, participants are not charged, and the images appear only inside the study and its public demonstration. They do not appear on the home page or in promotional material. Sahapedia's articles "Kalamkari: An Introduction" and "Kalamkari Woodblock Makers" are already among the cited sources for our Machilipatnam Kalamkari content.

We would like to show this photograph from the "Kalamkari" image gallery (https://www.sahapedia.org/kalamkari, slide 4):

- **6_IMG_0046.JPG**, captioned "Wooden blocks used for kalamkari printing in Machilipatnam" (https://www.sahapedia.org/sites/default/files/6_IMG_0046.JPG)

It would appear as a captioned figure beside the text on Machilipatnam printing blocks, at most 768 pixels on the longest side, uncropped and unaltered, with the credit "Photo: [photographer] / Sahapedia, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0". The figure would be identical in both versions of the study, and used for up to 5 years from first publication.

Your pages state that content is available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 "unless otherwise specified", while section 9 of your Terms of Use grants use of the website "for personal uses only". Could you please confirm:

1. that this photograph is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and that showing it inside our non-commercial research study, as described above, is permitted;
2. the name of the photographer, so that we can credit them correctly (the "Photograph by" field on the gallery is empty).

If possible, we would also be grateful for higher-resolution copies of these photographs from "Kalamkari: An Introduction" (https://www.sahapedia.org/kalamkari-introduction), whose published files are 448 pixels wide, on the same terms:

- "A well-used wooden block with a printed textile, Machilipatnam";
- "Printer's workshop, Machilipatnam";
- "Washing sub-unit, Machilipatnam" (we understand this one is courtesy of Shatarupa Bhattacharyya and would need her permission).
