# Request to the V&A: use of three collection images in a non-commercial research study

**To:** vaimages@vam.ac.uk
**From:** cxa.craftecosystem@gmail.com
**Subject:** Non-commercial academic use of three collection images in a learning study (IIT Kharagpur)

Dear V&A Images team,

We are a research team at the Centre of Excellence for Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. Our study (ethics approval CXA/ETH/01) compares two ways of presenting knowledge about Indian hand-block-printed textiles to learners:

- a conventional web page;
- a virtual museum that participants walk through on a computer screen.

The platform is non-commercial. Nothing is sold, participants are not charged, and the images appear only inside the study and its public demonstration. They do not appear on the home page or in promotional material.

We would like to show these three images, downloaded from Explore the Collections:

- **5473A/(IS)** (O469296), pieces of an ajrakh textile, Sind
- **IS.1765-1883** (O164855), textile, kalamkari and block printing, Machilipatnam
- **4497(IS)** (O481213), wrapped garment, probably Sanganer

Each would appear as a captioned figure, at most 768 pixels on the longest side, credited "© Victoria and Albert Museum, London". The figures would be identical in both versions of the study, and used for up to 5 years from first publication. One image would be cropped slightly to remove the museum's paper tag.

Your terms permit non-commercial websites and academic e-publications. They place "exhibitions, displays and catalogues" under commercial conditions. Could you please confirm whether showing these figures inside our virtual-museum research environment counts as non-commercial academic website use under section 3?

With thanks,

Saurav Kumar, Ankit Kumar and Priyadarshi Patnaik
Centre of Excellence for Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, IIT Kharagpur
