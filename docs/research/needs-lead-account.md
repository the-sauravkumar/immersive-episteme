## Open Points Before Buying From Fab

- Epic's docs say the Personal tier is "For buyers who have not generated more than $100,000 USD in gross revenue from commercial activity in the last 12 months." (dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fab/licenses-and-pricing-in-fab, section Licenses). Which side IIT KGP falls on is not settled.
- The Fab EULA returned 403, so whether Fab source files may sit in this project's public repo is open. Check before any Fab file is committed or pushed (DESIGN 10.5).
- Two listings state Unreal format only (Animal Dung Vol 3, Photorealistic Wet Clay Ground), so they are left out above: buying them may give no file usable in a three.js build.
