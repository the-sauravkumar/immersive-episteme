# Note: permission to include the study images in the public source repository

The project lead (Saurav Kumar) asked the V&A and PARI whether the study's image files may be included in the project's public GitHub repository. He reports that both said yes by telephone:

- the V&A, through its contact and help desk;
- PARI, through its contact and help desk.

The question covered:

- the V&A textile images (`scripts/content/study-photos/*-768.jpg`, at most 768 px, credited "© Victoria and Albert Museum, London"), permitted in `va-image-licence-reply.md`;
- the PARI photographs, permitted in `pari-image-permission-reply.md`, credited "Photo: Rahul Maganti / People's Archive of Rural India (PARI). CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, used with permission."

**Status:** confirmed orally. No written confirmation is on file. If either organisation later sends one in writing, add it here word for word.

The other image sources need no separate permission for the public repository:

- the Flickr images are under Creative Commons licences and keep their credits;
- the Sahapedia permission covers "reproducing, hosting, displaying and distributing the photograph as reasonably necessary for the study" (`sahapedia-image-permission-reply.md`).
