# Request to PARI: use of two article photographs in a non-commercial research study

**To:** zahra@ruralindiaonline.org
**Cc:** namita@ruralindiaonline.org
**From:** cxa.craftecosystem@gmail.com
**Subject:** Non-commercial academic use of two photographs from "A muted maggam, a bleeding block print" in a learning study (IIT Kharagpur)

Dear PARI team,

We are a research team at the Centre of Excellence for Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. Our study (ethics approval CXA/ETH/01) compares two ways of presenting knowledge about Indian hand-block-printed textiles to learners:

- a conventional web page;
- a virtual museum that participants walk through on a computer screen.

The platform is non-commercial. Nothing is sold, participants are not charged, and the images appear only inside the study and its public demonstration. They do not appear on the home page or in promotional material. We are not republishing the article.

We would like to show two photographs by Rahul Maganti from "A muted maggam, a bleeding block print" (27 January 2017, https://ruralindiaonline.org/en/articles/a-muted-maggam-a-bleeding-block-print/):

- **Photo 04**, "The fabric is put into boiling water with raw leaves to give the Kalamkari prints better colour and texture"
- **Photo 06**, "Kalamkari artisans creating block prints in a workshop in Pedana"

Each would appear as a captioned figure, at most 768 pixels on the longest side, uncropped and unaltered, credited "Photo: Rahul Maganti / People's Archive of Rural India (PARI), CC BY-NC-ND 4.0", or in any other manner PARI specifies. The figures would be identical in both versions of the study, and used for up to 5 years from first publication. The artisans would not be named.

PARI's copyright page licenses PARI Content under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and says this is shown by an icon on the relevant pages. The article page does not carry that icon, so we would like to check before using the photographs. Could you please confirm:

1. that these two photographs are PARI Content under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, or, if not, grant permission for the use described above;
2. the credit line PARI would like us to use.

If possible, we would also be grateful for the original, larger files of photos 04 and 06, and of photo 07 ("An empty Kalamkari workshed on a rainy day …"), on the same terms. The published files are 1,400 pixels wide (photo 07 is 824 × 1,120).

With thanks,

Saurav Kumar, Ankit Kumar and Priyadarshi Patnaik
Centre of Excellence for Unified AI-Enabled Craft Ecosystem Platform, IIT Kharagpur
