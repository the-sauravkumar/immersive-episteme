Date: 2026-11-07. Adopted under the lead's delegation (DR-059 route; DR-120).

## Standard

- An item is `verified` only when every quote it carries was re-found verbatim, at its stated page or section, in the archived text of a source this file already records, and the claim or explanation says no more than its quotes.
- Where a quote did not support the claim as worded, the supporting passage was looked for in the same recorded sources. If one was found it was added; otherwise the claim was narrowed to what the source says, or the item was removed.
- No source was added to the file. No fact was added: every changed claim or explanation repeats or narrows what its quotes say.
- Normalisation (citation policy): ligatures, curly quotes and dashes, line-end hyphenation and whitespace only (`verify_quotes.py`). Scanned-PDF OCR noise in S1 is kept exactly as extracted; it is part of the verbatim text.

## Method and Tools

- `content/review/tools/evidence/source_archive.py`: reads the manifest, loads a source folder and splits each source into located segments with the V11 segmenter (`segment_sources.py`, now importable): one segment per PDF page (`PDF p. N`), one per web-page section (`§ "<heading>"`).
- `content/review/tools/evidence/verify_from_manifest.py <file> <archive folder>`: re-finds every claim and relationship quote at its locator, reports any cited source without a passage and any relationship quote over 25 words, and exits non-zero on any failure.
- `content/review/tools/find-in-source.py <file> <archive folder> <source id> <regex>`: searches one archived source by located segment (rewritten for the current manifest; it pointed at a folder layout that no longer exists).
- `content/review/tools/evidence/apply_bagru_verification.py`: applies the changes below. Every passage it adds is cut from the archived text between two literal anchors, so it cannot be typed by hand; it refuses a relationship whose passages do not cover its sources, or one over 25 words.

Results before the changes: 253 of 253 claim passages verbatim at their locators; 0 of 116 relationship citations had a passage. After: 268 of 268 claim passages and 110 of 110 relationship passages verbatim at their locators, every cited source covered (`verify_from_manifest.py` exit 0).

### Passages Added or Dropped on Claims

A passage was added where a claim said more than its quotes (for example the Kharadiya block makers, the wooden handle, the 3 to 6 days of scouring, the copper vessel and sun-drying in S7, aligning blocks by eye, the eleven-motif list, the 2024 GI sentence in S8). A passage was dropped where it did not bear on the claim (for example a page-2 'Base colour is off white' under the colour tray, the Wikipedia footer under the GI date, a chalk sentence under the water claim). Every concept still carries a passage for every source each claim cites.

### Relationships

- All 78 remaining relationships now carry one passage (at most 25 words) per cited source; 22 explanations and one condition were narrowed to what the passages say (including the one below).
- `bagru.tradition.bagru-hand-block-print AFFECTED_BY bagru.environmental_condition.semi-arid-seasonality` cited S1, S2, S4, S7 and S9, none of which describe the seasons; its explanation came from S6. It now cites S6, which this file already records, with that passage.
- Removed: `bagru.environmental_condition.groundwater-and-effluent AFFECTED_BY bagru.process_stage.final-washing` (was `needs_evidence`). No recorded source ties groundwater pollution to the final washing. It is archived with its reason in `content/review/removed-relationships.json`.

Bagru's stages, tools and links all are, so the Bagru transfer items lost the mark and the immediate transfer form is no longer a demo form (`test-transfer`, battery v7). The delayed transfer form draws on Dabu and keeps the mark until Dabu passes the same check.
