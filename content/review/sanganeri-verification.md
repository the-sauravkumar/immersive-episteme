## Method

- **Tool:** `content/review/tools/evidence/verify_tradition.py <tradition.json> <archive dir> <out.json>`, extended for this tradition:
  - `PDF p. N`: the quote must be on page N alone. Pages come from one `pdftotext` run split at form feeds (xpdf 4.06 returns success for pages past the end, so the per-page loop never stopped).
  - Each PDF page is read in two output encodings, UTF-8 and Latin1. The S1 GI statement of case is a scanned PDF whose text layer maps some glyphs outside Latin-1: UTF-8 output gives `r€move` and `„Beggar‟`, Latin1 output gives `rmove` and `,,Beggar`, which is the text the 2026-10-14 extraction produced and the quotes were taken from. Both renderings are the same archived file; no character is substituted.
  - `PDF pp. N-M`: the quote must be in pages N to M read as one run (two S10 quotes run across a page break).
  - `Abstract ...`: the quote must be in the abstract of the archived OpenAlex record (S11, whose DOI returns 404).
  - `§ "<heading>"`: the quote must be inside the section that follows that h1-h4 heading in the archived HTML.
  - Normalisation is `norm()` from `verify_quotes.py` only: ligatures, quote marks, dashes, soft hyphens, line-end hyphenation and whitespace.
  - Each claim also gets the list of its content words that none of its quotes contain. Every claim and every relationship explanation was then read against its quotes.
- **Archive:** `D:/Work/IIT-KGP/Paper_1-artifacts/sources/sanganeri-2026-11-07/` with `archive.json`. The copies are those of the 2026-10-16 whole-tradition check (`scratchpad-archive/corpus-check/sanganeri-work/live/`); the four PDFs and three V&A pages hash equal to `source-manifest.tsv`. The 2026-10-14 archive of the other pages is no longer on disk, so those copies hash differently; the quotes were found in them.
