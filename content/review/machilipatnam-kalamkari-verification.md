Every claim and every relationship was checked only against the 16 sources this file already records, each at its page or section, with a verbatim quote that a script finds in the archived source text. ## Method

- **Tool:** `content/review/tools/evidence/verify_tradition.py <tradition.json> <archive dir> <out.json>`, the Sanganeri version, extended for this tradition:
  - `§ "<heading>"` now also matches a bold or italic subheading: a paragraph or line break holding only bold or italic text, the way S12, S13, S15 and the V&A record pages mark their sections (`<p><strong>The original art form</strong></p>`, `<p><em>The Dye</em></p>`, `<br><b>Materials & Making</b><br>`). The section runs to the next heading of either kind. h1-h4 sections are read as before, so occurrence counts are unchanged.
  - An archive entry `"pages": "<dir>"` holds `p1.txt` to `pN.txt`, the per-page text of a PDF that is no longer served, checked like a PDF's pages. S7's PDF (IJTK, NISCPR) returns 404 at its recorded URL and at its DOI; its pages are the 2026-10-16 `pdftotext` extraction of the copy whose hash equals `source-manifest.tsv`.
  - Everything else is unchanged: `PDF p. N` on that page alone, in UTF-8 and Latin1 renderings; `PDF pp. N-M` as one run; normalisation by `norm()` from `verify_quotes.py` only.

## Changes Made

Claims reworded to what their quotes say:

- `tradition.machilipatnam-kalamkari #1`: Pedana is '13 km' from Machilipatnam (S3), not 'about 13 km'.
- `design_principle.persian-floral-motifs #1`: 'due to Mughal rule and trade with Persia' is now S7's 'due to the influence of Mughal rule and trade proximity with Persia and Iran'.
- `process_stage.curing-drying #1`: the printed cloth dries in sunlight 'for one day' (S3), not 'about a day'.
- `environmental_condition.flowing-water-washing #2`: 'Pond washing takes two hours' (S15); the villages turning makers away are named (Pedapulipaka and Sitarampuram) and their reason is the villagers' allegation that chemical colours pollute their water, not a fact about 'chemically printed cloth'.
- `environmental_condition.effluent-pollution #0`: the Krishna District Collector 'sent out a few notices to those using chemical colours, warning them of action' (S15); the claim said he issued notices to units.

Quotes and locators:

- S7 p. 1: the Machilipatnam sentence runs across the page footer ('*Corresponding author'), so its quote was split into the two runs of text on either side (`persian-floral-motifs #1`, `comparison.vs-srikalahasti #0`).
