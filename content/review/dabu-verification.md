## Method

- **Tool:** `content/review/tools/evidence/verify_tradition.py <tradition.json> <archive dir> <out.json>` (pdftotext from poppler, MINGW64 build). Normalisation is `norm()` from `verify_quotes.py`: ligatures, quote marks, dashes, soft hyphens, line-end hyphenation and whitespace only.
  - `PDF p. N`: the quote must be on page N alone (`pdftotext -f N -l N`).
  - `§ "<heading>"`: the quote must be inside the section that follows that h1-h4 heading in the archived HTML (the k-th one when `(occurrence k of n)` is given).
  - A claim's own `quote` field must be found in one of the claim's cited sources (this check was added to the tool for this round).
  - Each claim also gets the list of its content words that none of its quotes contain. Every claim was then read against its quotes; a claim wider than its quotes was reworded to what the source says.
- **Archive:** each recorded source URL was fetched again on 2026-11-07 (curl, browser user agent, decompression on). The copies are in `D:/Work/IIT-KGP/Paper_1-artifacts/sources/dabu-2026-11-07/` with `archive.json`. The 2026-10-14 copies are no longer on disk, so pages whose served HTML has changed since then hash differently from `source-manifest.tsv`; the quotes were found in the new copies.
- **Search:** passages for new quotes were found with a regular-expression search over the same section split the verifier uses, then placed and re-checked by the verifier.

## Stage Order (PRECEDES)

The cloth sequence rests on S18 (Rao 2019), whose "The Process" passage names the stages one after another. The offset of each stage's phrase in the archived § "Dabu Printing" section increases strictly in the order of the PRECEDES links (ordinals 1-8). S3 (Craft Revival Trust, Deesa) confirms stamping, sawdust, sun-drying, dip-dyeing and repeating in the same order; S19 confirms fast-dye printing before the mud resist. The paste-making step (mud-soaking before paste-preparation) rests on S18 and S15, which both describe mud soaked overnight and then made into a sticky paste.
