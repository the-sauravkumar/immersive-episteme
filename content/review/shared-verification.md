# Shared Craft Science: Source Verification (2026-11-08)

Evidence table for `content/shared/craft-science.json`, verified under the lead's delegation (DR-122) by the method of the six DR-120 tradition checks. Every claim and every relationship was checked only against the 17 sources this file already records, each at its page or section, with a verbatim quote that a script finds in the archived source text. No source was added.

## Why

Graph rule KG206 (`src/features/knowledge/graph/rules.ts`) refuses an accepted relationship with an unaccepted endpoint. DR-120 verified the six tradition files but not this file, which defines the shared mechanisms, materials and outcomes they link to, so 31 links from five traditions to these concepts stayed `under_review` (Ajrakh 9, Bagh 3, Dabu 6, Machilipatnam Kalamkari 6, Sanganeri 7), and Bagru's 8, set `verified`, failed the content test's endpoint check. This check closes that gap at its root.

## Result

- **Quotes:** 191 of 191 found verbatim at their locator (before the changes below: 182 of 195).
- **Claims:** 90 in 24 concepts, unchanged in number; every cited source of every claim has at least one quote, and every claim was read against its quotes.
- **Concepts:** 24 verified (20 were under_review, 4 needs_evidence).
- **Relationships:** 24 (25 before: 1 removed), all verified. Every cited source of every relationship has its one quote of at most 25 words.
- **Tradition links:** the 31 KG206-held links of Ajrakh, Bagh, Dabu, Machilipatnam Kalamkari and Sanganeri were read again against their own quotes, which `verify_tradition.py` (or, for Bagh, `verify_tradition_tsv.py`) finds again at their locators, and set to `verified`, each explanation narrowed to its quote. Each tradition's record lists them under "DR-122".

## Method

- **Tool:** `content/review/tools/evidence/verify_tradition.py <file> <archive dir> <out.json>`, the tool of the Machilipatnam Kalamkari check, with two faults found and fixed here:
  - the script, style, noscript and svg stripping held a backspace and a control character where `\b` and `\1` belong (a non-raw write), so it never matched and script text stayed in the page sections;
  - the section splitter returned the captured heading text as a section of its own, so every heading counted twice under its name and `(occurrence k of n)` locators counted those empty copies (seen on Dabu S2 `§ "History:"`). Sections are now cut between heading matches, so counts are real again.
  After the fix all five archived traditions still pass in full: Ajrakh 511 of 511, Dabu 419 of 419, Machilipatnam Kalamkari 531 of 531, Sanganeri 448 of 448, and Bagh (`verify_tradition_tsv.py`) 406 at locator with no problem.
- **Archive:** `D:/Work/IIT-KGP/Paper_1-artifacts/sources/shared-2026-11-08/` with `archive.json` and `sha256.txt`, fetched on 2026-11-08 from the recorded URLs. S1, S2 (the 197 MB thesis), S3, S5, S6, S7, S8, S11, S13 and S15 hash equal to `source-manifest.tsv`; S4, S9, S10, S12, S14, S16 and S17 (the arXiv PDF) are live pages or files that changed bytes, and every quote was found in them.
- Normalisation is `norm()` from `verify_quotes.py` only.
