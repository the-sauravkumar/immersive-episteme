## Method

- **Tool:** `content/review/tools/evidence/verify_tradition.py <tradition.json> <archive dir> <out.json>` (PDFTOTEXT names the pdftotext binary; xpdf 4.06 was used). It uses `norm()` from `verify_quotes.py`, so normalisation is limited to ligatures, quote marks, dashes, soft hyphens, line-end hyphenation and whitespace.
  - `PDF p. N`: the quote must be on page N alone (`pdftotext -f N -l N`).
  - `§ "<heading>"`: the quote must be inside the section that follows that h1-h4 heading in the archived HTML.
  - A quote that fails is searched for in the whole source, so a wrong locator is reported with the place the text really is.
  - Each claim also gets the list of its content words that none of its quotes contain. Every claim was then read against its quotes; a claim wider than its quotes was reworded to what the source says.
- **Archive:** each recorded source URL was fetched again on 2026-11-07 (S1 and S2 share the registry page). The GI Journal No. 188 copy of application 1050 is the PDF used by the 2026-10-16 check. The copies are in `D:/Work/IIT-KGP/Paper_1-artifacts/sources/ajrakh-2026-11-07/` with `archive.json`. The 2026-10-14 archive of the HTML pages is no longer on disk, so pages whose served HTML has changed since then hash differently from `source-manifest.tsv`; the quotes were found in the new copies.
